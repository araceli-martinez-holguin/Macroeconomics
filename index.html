Course Overview

This repository contains the computational materials developed for the graduate Macroeconomics course in the Master in Economics program at the School of Economics, UNAM.

The course combines macroeconomic theory with numerical methods, simulations, data analysis, and computational experiments. The objective is to use computational tools not simply to calculate solutions, but to explore the mechanisms underlying macroeconomic models and their implications for economic policy.

Laboratories are designed around a simple principle: move from the economic model to the computational experiment, and from the computational result back to economic interpretation.

Instructor

Araceli Martínez-Holguín is a PhD student in Economics at the School of Economics of the National Autonomous University of Mexico (UNAM).

Topics Covered

01

Macroeconomic Foundations

Core macroeconomic concepts, equilibrium conditions, and analytical frameworks.

02

Keynesian Macroeconomics

Aggregate demand, income determination, the IS-LM framework, comparative statics, and macroeconomic policy.

03

Growth Theory

Quantitative implementations of growth models, including Solow and Ramsey-Cass-Koopmans frameworks.

04

Business Cycle Models

Dynamic macroeconomic models, simulations, and the quantitative analysis of fluctuations.

05

New Keynesian Macroeconomics

Inflation, output, monetary policy, expectations, and dynamic adjustment.

06

Macroeconometrics

Time-series methods, filters, and VAR models for empirical macroeconomic analysis.

Computational approach. The repository uses R to translate theoretical models into numerical experiments, simulations, visualizations, and reproducible analyses.
